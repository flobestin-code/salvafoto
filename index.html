FotoKeep

Apple Photos backup to a drive you own — iPhone, iPad and Mac.

Support

Questions, bug reports and feature requests: solano@tuta.io. In the app, Settings → Self-check can prepare an email with a diagnostic report for you.

Common questions

Which drives work on iPhone? USB-C or Lightning flash drives, SSDs and SD card readers formatted as APFS or exFAT, and network shares that appear in the Files app.

Can I read the archive without FotoKeep? Yes. Originals are plain files in folders by year and month.

Does anything go to the cloud? No. FotoKeep has no server and makes no network requests.

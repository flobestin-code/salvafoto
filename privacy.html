FotoKeep Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026

FotoKeep copies the original photos and videos from your Apple Photos library to a drive or folder you choose. It is built so that your media never leaves your own devices.

What FotoKeep does not do

FotoKeep collects no personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting service and no server. It does not upload photos, videos or any information about them to the internet.

What FotoKeep accesses on your device

Photos library. With your permission, FotoKeep reads your library to copy originals to the drive you selected, and adds items back to Photos only when you ask it to restore them. If you use “Free Up Space”, it asks Photos to delete items whose archived copy it has verified; Photos keeps them in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

The drive or folder you choose. FotoKeep writes the archive, a catalog file and an operation log there. These files belong to you and can be read or deleted without the app.

App settings are stored locally on the device.

Children

FotoKeep collects no data from anyone, including children.

Contact

Questions about privacy or this policy: solano@tuta.io.

Политика конфиденциальности

FotoKeep копирует оригиналы фото и видео из медиатеки «Фото» на выбранный вами диск или в папку. Приложение не собирает персональные данные, не использует аккаунты, аналитику, рекламу и серверы и ничего не отправляет в интернет. Доступ к «Фото» нужен, чтобы скопировать оригиналы и — только по вашей команде — вернуть их обратно или удалить проверенные копии из медиатеки (они остаются в «Недавно удалённых» 30 дней). Архив, каталог и журнал лежат на вашем диске и принадлежат вам. Вопросы: solano@tuta.io.

Informativa sulla privacy

FotoKeep copia gli originali di foto e video dalla libreria Foto sul disco o nella cartella che scegli. L’app non raccoglie dati personali, non usa account, analisi, pubblicità né server e non invia nulla su internet. L’accesso a Foto serve per copiare gli originali e — solo su tua richiesta — per riportarli in Foto o eliminare dalla libreria gli elementi la cui copia è stata verificata (restano in “Eliminati di recente” per 30 giorni). Archivio, catalogo e registro si trovano sul tuo disco e appartengono a te. Domande: solano@tuta.io.

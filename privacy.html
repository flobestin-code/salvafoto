SalvaFoto Privacy Policy

Effective 21 September 2026 · applies to SalvaFoto for iPhone, iPad and Mac

In short: SalvaFoto collects no data about you. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no crash-reporting service, and it sends nothing to us. Your photos move only between your device and a drive you choose.

1. Who is responsible

SalvaFoto is developed and published by an independent developer, who is the data controller for the purposes of this policy. Contact: solano@tuta.io.

2. Data we collect

None. We do not collect, receive, store or sell any personal data, usage data, identifiers, diagnostics or location (a photo’s own place of capture stays in the archive on your drive, see §4). The app contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not use Apple's advertising identifier. Because nothing reaches us, there is nothing we could share with anyone.

3. What the app accesses on your device, and why

  • Photos library (with your permission) — to read original photos and videos and copy them to your drive; to add items back to Photos when you ask for a restore; and, only when you use “Free Up Space” and confirm it, to delete items whose archived copy has been verified. Deleted items stay in Photos’ Recently Deleted album for 30 days.
  • The drive or folder you choose — to write the archive. On Mac and iPhone the system grants access only to the location you picked; SalvaFoto remembers that choice with a system bookmark.
  • Notifications (optional) — local notifications that a backup finished. They are generated on the device; nothing is sent through a push server.
  • Live Activities (iPhone) — backup progress on the Lock Screen, produced on the device.
  • Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode — asked before files are erased from your drive for good. The check is done by the system; the app only learns whether it succeeded and never receives any biometric data.

4. What is written to your drive

Next to the copied originals, SalvaFoto keeps a catalog file and an operation log on your drive. The catalog describes each archived item so the app can avoid duplicates and verify copies. It contains the file name, dates, size, checksum, dimensions, media type, favourite and hidden flags, album names, the location where the photo was taken (if the photo has one) and the name of the device that made the backup. This is the same information your photos already carry; it stays on your drive and is readable by anyone who has the drive. The archive is not encrypted by SalvaFoto — if that matters to you, use a drive formatted as encrypted APFS.

5. Data stored in the app

Your settings and the location of your drive are stored locally on the device and are removed when you delete the app.

6. Network

SalvaFoto has no servers of its own and sends nothing to us. The only connections are made by Apple: the App Store, when you buy the full version or restore the purchase, and Photos (below). Links in the app (this policy, support, email) open in your browser or mail app. If your library uses iCloud Photos, Photos may download originals from iCloud while SalvaFoto reads them; that transfer is performed by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy.

7. Keeping and deleting your data

Everything SalvaFoto creates is on your own devices and drives, under your control. To remove it, delete the SalvaFoto folder from your drive and delete the app. We hold no copy to delete.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the EU/UK GDPR or California law), you have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or port personal data and to object to its processing. Since we process no personal data, we will have nothing to return or delete, but you are welcome to write to us, and you may lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Children

SalvaFoto collects no data from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent age in your country).

10. Changes

If the app ever begins to handle data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.

11. Contact

solano@tuta.io
